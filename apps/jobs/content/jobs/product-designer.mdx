## About the role

This is a fictional posting — a starting point for your own careers page, not a real opening. In
it, you'd design features customers actually enjoy using, partnering with engineering and support
to turn feedback into clear, simple interfaces.

## What you'd do

- Design flows and interfaces for new and existing features
- Run quick usability checks and iterate on feedback
- Maintain the shared design system alongside engineering

## What we'd look for

- A portfolio of shipped product work
- Comfort working directly in a design system, not just mockups
- A habit of writing down the "why" behind a design decision
