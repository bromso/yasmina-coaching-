## Booking and confirmation

When you book a time in the calendar or a seat in a group, the booking is confirmed at once. You receive a confirmation by email with the time, the place, a reference number and a link where you manage the booking.

The calendar shows times from 24 hours ahead and up to 90 days ahead. If you want to meet later than that, get in touch through the [contact form](www:/kontakt).

A request for a workshop, programme or talk is not yet a booking. You suggest up to three dates, and Yasmina comes back with a proposed time and a written quote. The booking applies only once you have accepted both. You then receive a new confirmation with a new link, and the link from the request stops working.

Before you book, you tick a box to say you have read the terms and the privacy policy. We record which version of the terms you accepted, which is the date of the latest change at the bottom of this page, and when you accepted them.

The link in the confirmation shows your sessions and their status. There you can cancel and, in a programme, book the next session. Anyone who has the link can do the same, so do not share it.

## API keys and webhooks

With a paid plan, a workspace's admins and owners can create API keys, which let other programs read and write in the workspace, and webhooks, which send events in the workspace to an address you choose.

- A workspace can hold at most 20 active API keys. A key can only do what you have given it permission to do. It is shown once, when it is created, and is valid for 30, 90 or 365 days, or until you revoke it.
- A workspace can hold at most 10 webhooks, and they are only sent to https addresses. A webhook is turned off after five dead deliveries in a row, each one still failing after every retry, or when the receiver answers that the address no longer exists.

You are responsible for keeping keys and secrets to yourselves and for what is done with them. Revoke a key at once if it may have ended up in the wrong hands.
