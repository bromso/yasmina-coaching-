When you book a session, take a seat at a group event or send a booking request, we keep what you enter: your name, email address, phone number if you give one, your organisation if you book for an employer, what you book, the time or the dates you prefer, the location, the number of participants and the venue if you give them, and your own notes. ### Visitor statistics

We count visits without cookies. Each page view is stored with the part of the site and the page, the site the visitor came from and a visitor id. The visitor id is a hash of the IP address and browser together with a random value that changes every day. So we can count visitors per day but not follow anyone from one day to the next, and the IP address and browser are never stored. If you have accepted all cookies and are signed in, the page view also gets your user id and your workspace's id. Page views from browsers that send Do Not Track are not counted.

- **Purpose:** to understand how the site is used and improve it.
- **Legal basis:** [TO FILL: legal basis for visitor statistics, suggested legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f)]
- **How long:** single page views for 90 days. The random value is deleted the day after. Daily totals, with nothing about individual visitors, are kept with no end date.

A note created with an API key is stored without a user id, and plan changes are stored with the workspace's id only. ## Cookies

A cookie is a small text file the site stores in your browser. Under Chapter 9, Section 28 of the Swedish Electronic Communications Act (2022:482), we may store something in your browser only if you have been told why and have agreed, unless it is needed for a service you have explicitly asked for.

We set only our own cookies, and each one is needed for the site to work. The visitor statistics work without cookies. Your choice in the cookie banner affects two things: if you accept all, page views get your user id when you are signed in, and error reports get your browser's user agent. Account events are stored with your user id whatever you choose (see Account events above).

The table also covers what we keep in the browser's local storage and cache.

| Name | What it does | How long | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `session` | Keeps you signed in. Shared between our sites. | 30 days | Necessary |
| `workspace` | Remembers which workspace you are working in. | 1 year | Necessary |
| `locale` | Remembers the language you chose. | 1 year | Necessary |
| `consent` | Remembers what you chose in the cookie banner. | 180 days | Necessary |
| `two_factor` | Keeps track of the sign-in while you enter your two-step verification code. | 5 minutes | Necessary |
| `oauth` | Protects the sign-in when you sign in with another service. | 10 minutes | Necessary |
| `admin_session` | Used only by our administrators while they give support signed in as a user. | 1 hour | Necessary |
| `theme` (local storage) | Remembers whether you chose a light or dark theme. Saved only once you choose. | Until you clear the browser's storage | Necessary |
| Page cache (service worker) | Keeps copies of pages you visited on the website, the blog and the legal pages, so they can be read offline. In the app, sign-in and account pages only program files are cached, never pages with your data. | Pages for 7 days, program files and images for 30 days | Necessary |
