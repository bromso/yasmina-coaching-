## Sign up

Go to the sign-up page and enter your email and password. You can also sign up with Google,
GitHub or Apple, or ask for a one-time sign-in link by email instead of choosing a password.

## Verify your email

After you sign up we send a verification link to your inbox. It's valid for 24 hours. Following
it confirms your address — most of the product works before you do, but you'll need a verified
email to create a workspace or turn on two-step verification.

If the link expires or you can't find the email, see
[I didn't get the email](/troubleshooting/i-didnt-get-the-email).
