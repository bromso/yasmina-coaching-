## Send an invitation

Open **Account**, go to the **Workspace** page and enter the person's email. Choose a role —
viewer, member or admin (owner is set separately, by transferring ownership). See
[Roles and permissions](/workspaces-billing/roles-and-permissions) for what each one can do.

The invitation link is valid for 7 days and is bound to the email address you sent it to: the
person has to sign in — or create an account — with exactly that address, verified, to accept it
(see [Create an account](/getting-started/create-an-account)). Re-inviting the same address
replaces the earlier link.

## Seat limits

Each plan has a seat limit — Free holds 3 members, Pro 10, Team is unlimited. Members count
towards the limit; a pending invitation does not. Once a workspace is full, both sending a new
invitation and accepting one already sent are refused, until a seat opens up or the workspace
upgrades. See [Change your plan](/workspaces-billing/change-your-plan).
