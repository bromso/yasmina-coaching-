## Check your spam folder first

Mail from us can land in spam or promotions, especially the first time. Search for our name
rather than scrolling, and double-check you typed your address correctly when you signed up — a
typo there means the mail went somewhere else entirely.

## Resend the verification email

You can sign in even before your email is verified. Once signed in, open **Account**, go to
**Personal info** and choose **Resend verification email**. You can ask for a new link up to 3
times every 15 minutes.

If you're trying to reset a forgotten password or sign in with a one-time link instead, those
mails are rate-limited the same way — wait a few minutes and try again rather than requesting
several in a row.
