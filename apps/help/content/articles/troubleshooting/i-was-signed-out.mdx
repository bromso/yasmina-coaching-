## Sessions last 30 days

Signing in keeps you signed in for 30 days from the moment you sign in, whatever you do in
between — just using the product doesn't restart the clock. After 30 days you'll just need to
sign in again — nothing is wrong, and nothing is lost.

## What can sign you out early

A few things end a session on purpose. Some end only your *other* sessions, leaving the one
you're using alone; others end every session at once, including the one you're using right now:

- Changing your password, or choosing **Sign out everywhere else** on the Security page in
  **Account** — your other sessions only.
- Signing a single device out from the device list on that same Security page — just that one.
- Resetting a forgotten password — every session, including this one.
- Requesting account deletion — every session at once, including this one; see
  [Export or delete your data](/account-security/export-or-delete-your-data).
- A superadmin resetting your two-step verification after you lost access to it, or revoking your
  sessions directly — every session.
- A superadmin suspending your account — every session. Trying to sign in again shows "This
  account is suspended. Contact support." instead of letting you in; reach out below and we'll
  look into it.

If none of those sound familiar, see
[Turn on two-step verification](/account-security/turn-on-two-step-verification) to add an extra
check to future sign-ins — or contact us below so we can look into it.
