## Change it

Open **Account**, go to **Personal info** and find the **Email address** card. Enter your new
address; if your account has a password, enter it too, and if you've turned on two-step
verification, enter a current code. You'll need to confirm it's really you, the same as for
[turning on two-step verification](/account-security/turn-on-two-step-verification).

We email a confirm link to the **new** address — that link is what makes the change, so open your
new inbox and select **Confirm new email**. Until you do, nothing changes: you can keep signing in
with your old address, and selecting **Send again** on the same card sends a fresh link.

## If you didn't request it

We also email your **current** address the moment a change is requested, with a **Cancel this
change** link, in case it wasn't you. Once a change is confirmed, we email your old address again
with a link that undoes it — good for 7 days.

Select that undo link if you didn't make the change yourself. It restores your old address, signs
every device out (including whichever one made the change), and removes anything set up on the
account since the change was requested: any sign-in provider connected, two-step verification
turned on, and a deletion, if one was scheduled. If your account has a password, we clear it and
email a link to set a new one. Either way, check your connected sign-in providers and two-step
verification afterwards — see
[turn on two-step verification](/account-security/turn-on-two-step-verification).
