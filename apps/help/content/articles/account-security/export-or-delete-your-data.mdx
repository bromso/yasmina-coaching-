## Export your data

Open **Account**, go to **Data & privacy** and request an export. It's prepared in the
background; once it's ready we email you a download link. The link works for 7 days, after which
the export expires and you'd need to request a new one — you can only ask for one export per day.

## Delete your account

On the same page, type your account's email address to confirm and submit the delete form. This
needs a recent sign-in — within the last 10 minutes — or you'll be asked to sign in again first.
Submitting signs you out everywhere at once, including on this device.

Your account and data are deleted 7 days from now. Sign in before then to cancel — it shows a
banner with a **Cancel deletion** button that stops it. Workspaces where you are the only member
are deleted too, and any paid plan on them is cancelled.

If you're the only owner of a workspace that has other members, you'll need to transfer ownership
or delete those workspaces first — see
[Roles and permissions](/workspaces-billing/roles-and-permissions).
