## The four roles

Every workspace member has one of four roles, each including everything below it:

- **Viewer** — can see the workspace's notes.
- **Member** — can also create notes, and delete their own.
- **Admin** — can also delete anyone's notes, manage members, send invitations and change the
  workspace's settings.
- **Owner** — can also manage billing, transfer ownership and delete the workspace.

A workspace always keeps at least one owner, so the last owner can't leave, be removed, or be
demoted until ownership is transferred to someone else.

## Changing someone's role

An admin or owner can change another member's role on the **Workspace** page in **Account**, up
to admin — owner is handed over separately, with **Transfer ownership**, which makes the previous
owner an admin. See [Invite your team](/getting-started/invite-your-team) for adding new members
in the first place.
