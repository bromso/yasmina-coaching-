## Where to manage billing

Open **Account**, go to **Payments**. Only the workspace's owner can change its plan, open the
billing portal, or cancel — everyone else sees the current plan without those controls.

## Plans and seats

Free holds up to 3 seats, Pro up to 10, and Team is unlimited; see
[Roles and permissions](/workspaces-billing/roles-and-permissions) for who counts as a seat.
Upgrading also unlocks paid features like chat. Checkout and the billing portal are normally
hosted by our payment provider — you're redirected there and back, with nothing card-related
entered on this site — though on a test instance a plan change can also apply right away with no
payment at all.
