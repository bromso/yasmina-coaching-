are the way they are and what was true when each was written — paths, counts and commands inside
them may have moved on since.

Nothing here is open work: do not re-run a plan from this directory. Current state lives in the
code, `CLAUDE.md` and the READMEs; what comes next lives in [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md).
